## Overview
The ARC Purdue Robot Racing Car is a student-built autonomous vehicle developed for high-speed navigation and competition on closed tracks. This project is part of the RoboRacer (formerly F1Tenth) competition. The project integrates mechanical design, embedded systems, sensing, and control to achieve reliable autonomous driving performance.

## My Role
As a member of the ARC Purdue team, I contributed to the mechanical and systems-level development of the vehicle, with a focus on translating design concepts into a functional, testable platform.

## Technical Contributions
- Assisted in mechanical design and assembly of the chassis and mounting structures
- Integrated hardware components including actuators, sensors, and electronics
- Supported vehicle testing, debugging, and iterative improvements based on track performance
- Collaborated with subteams to ensure mechanical compatibility with electrical and control systems

## Results & Learning
- Gained hands-on experience with real-world robotics integration challenges
- Learned the importance of rapid iteration, tolerance management, and design-for-maintenance
- Developed teamwork and technical communication skills in a multidisciplinary environment
